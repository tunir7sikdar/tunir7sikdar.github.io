// approach

Three systems I designed when the default approach wouldn't scale.

Deloitte USI · Insurance

Onboarding 17 vendor feeds without breaking commission data

Problem

A greenfield Insurance platform needed to onboard multiple vendor feeds in different formats. Backfilling historical data for new sources risked duplicating or corrupting figures already used for agent payouts.

Approach

Architected a config-driven, idempotent ingestion framework with dedicated backfill logic, plus imputation and standardization rules to handle missing or malformed fields before they reached the lakehouse.

Outcome

4.2 TB of daily volume onboarded across all 17 sources with zero duplication or data loss on reprocessing, and a 99%+ on-time SLA for commission-critical reporting.

Fractal Analytics · Retail

Recurring data gaps breaking a retailer's BI reporting

Problem

A Fortune 500 retail client's inventory feeds arrived with missing SKU-level fields and inconsistent vendor formats, quietly breaking downstream BI reports.

Approach

Designed config-driven ETL pipelines with imputation logic for missing fields and automated backfill jobs for historical records, then restructured output into Delta tables with star/snowflake schema modeling.

Outcome

Reliable BI reporting at scale, with manual error-handling intervention down 60% and annual licensing costs cut by $150K.

Deloitte USI · Firm Initiative

Giving stakeholders real-time visibility into a batch-only system

Problem

Business stakeholders had no way to see mobile app usage as it happened — every pipeline feeding their dashboards was batch-only.

Approach

Led a team of 5 data engineers to design a real-time event ingestion pipeline using Spark Structured Streaming, feeding operational dashboards directly.

Outcome

Stakeholders could monitor live usage metrics and act on them, instead of waiting on the next day's batch run.
